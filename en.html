Explore · Fish · Record

Everything you need on the water, in one app

Built to be used right on the boat or at the lakeshore, with no internet connection.

My zone

GPS instantly detects which Quebec fishing zone you're in — computed on your phone, no signal needed.

Regulations

Seasons, catch limits, length limits and permitted gear for every zone, with a direct link to the government's official table.

Lakes & rivers

3,224 lakes and the 30 major salmon, landlocked-salmon and trout rivers, with municipality, zone and species present.

Bathymetry

Depth maps of major waters — Memphrémagog, Champlain, Mégantic, Saint-Jean and more — to find holes and shoals.

Species

31 regulated species, from walleye to muskellunge, with photos, habitat, size and fishing techniques.

Lures

A lure guide plus an assistant that suggests the right pick for the species, season and today's conditions.

Outfitters

429 fishing outfitters across Quebec: contact info, species fished, one-tap calling and directions in Maps.

Fishing log

Record your catches — species, spot, size, lure, photos — in a private log that never leaves your phone.

Tales & legends

Thirty Quebec fishing tales and legends to read by the campfire between outings.

The Captain's Log

Your best catches, kept for good

Every catch in your log becomes an elegant keepsake page — gold medallion, photo gallery, wax seal and signature — ready to save or post to Instagram and Facebook in one tap.

And your log stays yours: no account, no server. Your fishing spots stay secret.

Preview

A true outfitter's-lodge look

Navy, gold and copper: an app as handsome at the cabin as it is handy on the water.

Your location never leaves your phone. CaMordTu uses GPS only to find your fishing zone, right on the device. No account, no ads, no tracking — your honey holes stay between you and the lake.

A note on regulations. CaMordTu shows the general rules for each zone as published by the Quebec government. Some waters have exceptions: when in doubt, the app links you straight to the official table, which always has the final word.
