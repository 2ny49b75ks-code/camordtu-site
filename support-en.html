Frequently asked questions

Does the app work offline?

Yes. Fishing zones, regulations, lakes, rivers, depth maps, species, lures, outfitters and your fishing log are all available without an internet connection once the app is installed. Zone detection uses GPS, which works even with no cell coverage.

Why can't the app find my zone?

Make sure CaMordTu can access your location: Settings → CaMordTu → Location → “While Using the App”. Under dense forest cover, give the GPS a few seconds to settle.

Are my catches sent anywhere?

No. Your fishing log stays entirely on your device. See our privacy policy.

Are the regulations official?

CaMordTu includes the general rules for each zone as published by the Government of Quebec, with a direct link to the official table for exceptions specific to certain waters. When in doubt, the official table always has the final word. Regulations are updated in the app each new fishing season.

Is the app in English?

The app interface is currently in French. This website and our support are available in English.

Is there a subscription?

No. CaMordTu is a one-time App Store purchase ($6.99 CAD), with no subscription and no in-app purchases. Updates are included.
